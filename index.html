Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

The short version

Sourdough Idle does not collect, transmit or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics SDK. Your culture lives on your device.

What is stored, and where

  • Your save — the culture's name, age, buildings, loaves and settings. Written to your device's own storage (UserDefaults / IndexedDB). It is included in an iCloud device backup if you have one turned on, because that is how iOS backs up all app data. It is never sent to me.
  • Purchases — handled entirely by Apple. I receive anonymous receipt validation only; I never see your name, email or payment details.

Adverts

The game offers optional rewarded videos. If you watch one, the advertising network may collect device-level identifiers to serve and measure that advert, under its own privacy policy. You are never required to watch one, and the "Quiet mornings" purchase or a Guild membership removes them entirely.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. No personal information is knowingly collected from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Settings → Start a new culture erases the save. Deleting the app removes everything on the device. Since nothing is held on a server, there is nothing for me to delete on your behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
