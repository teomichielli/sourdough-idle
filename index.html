Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

The short version

Sourdough Idle has no accounts and no sign-in, and the game itself sends nothing anywhere — your culture lives on your device. Two third-party kits inside the app do collect data: Google AdMob, which shows the optional adverts, and RevenueCat, which checks purchases with Apple. What each one takes is set out below. I never sell anything to anybody.

What is stored, and where

  • Your save — the culture's name, age, buildings, loaves and settings. Written to your device's own storage. It is included in an iCloud device backup if you have one turned on, because that is how iOS backs up all app data. It is never sent to me.
  • Purchases — taken by Apple, then checked by RevenueCat, which tells the app which items you own so it can restore them. RevenueCat sees which items were bought and an anonymous identifier. It is never linked to your name, and I never see your payment details.

Adverts

Adverts are shown by Google AdMob, under Google's privacy policy. Almost all of them are rewarded videos you choose to watch; a single full-screen advert may appear at one of two stopping points — closing the summary after a long absence, or after dividing your culture — at most once every three minutes.

To serve and measure them, AdMob collects the following:

DataPurposeTracking
Device identifierThird-party advertising, analyticsYes
Advertising dataThird-party advertising, analyticsYes
Product interactionThird-party advertising, analyticsYes
Performance dataThird-party advertising, analyticsYes
Crash dataAnalyticsNo
Coarse locationThird-party advertisingNo

Coarse location is worked out from your IP address; the app never asks for or uses your device's location.

Some of the above counts as tracking under Apple's definition, which is why iOS asks your permission the first time you open the game. Saying no is fine — the game plays identically and you still get every reward. The "Quiet mornings" purchase or a Guild membership removes adverts entirely.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, three scores — your culture's age in days, loaves baked, and how far up the bread ladder you are — go to Apple's leaderboards under Apple's privacy policy. Signing in is optional and nothing in the game depends on it.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. No personal information is knowingly collected from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Settings → Start a new culture erases the save. Deleting the app removes everything on the device. Since I hold nothing on a server, there is nothing for me to delete on your behalf. To reset your advertising identifier, use iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
